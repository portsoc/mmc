      Using the Mission Model Canvas

      The canvas has nine sections ("Key Partners", "Key Activities", etc.) plus a title, description and team along the top.

      Editing

      Click a section and type. Everything saves automatically and appears for everyone on the canvas straight away.

      Click outside a section or press Escape to stop editing.

      Grey italic words such as title and team are only hints; just start typing.

      Working together

      The coloured circles at the top show who else is on the canvas right now.

      Share & invite in the ☰ menu gives you a read-only link or invites someone to edit (canvas owner only).

      Contributors shows who has written what.

      Versions

      Name this version… saves the canvas as it is now under a name you choose. A snapshot is also taken automatically each day.

      Version history lists every version. Click one to see it on the canvas, or press ▶ to watch the canvas develop. The canvas is read-only while you look; close the list to return to the current version. The owner can restore an old version, and the current one is saved first so nothing is lost.

      Focusing & Maximising

      Tap a section icon to open that section in an enlarged focus view. Tap the icon again, press Escape, tap outside the card, or swipe down to close it.

      Shift-click a section to fade it so the others stand out. Type in a faded section to bring it back. The page address remembers which sections are faded, so you can share a focused view.

      Settings

      Your account, sign out, and display options such as highlighting sections by contributor and playback speed.
